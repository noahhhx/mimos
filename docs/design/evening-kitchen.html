Red Lentil Dahl

You'll need

  • 2 cups red lentils
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 400 g canned tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp butter
kcal per serving
430
Protein
21 g
Carbs
62 g
Fat
9 g

Cheap, fast, and better on day two. Red lentils collapse into a soft, spoonable stew without any help.

Method

  1. Rinse the lentils until the water runs clear.
  2. Fry the onion in the oil until golden, then add the garlic, ginger, and spices for a minute.
  3. Add the lentils, tomatoes, and 4 cups of water. Simmer until the lentils have collapsed, 20–25 minutes.
  4. Swirl in the butter and season generously with salt.
  5. Serve with rice or bread and a dollop of yogurt.
